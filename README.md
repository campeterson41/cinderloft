# Cinderloft

A fast browser game: fly a house on balloons over a sea of lava. Every balloon you lose makes the house heavier.

Play it: https://campeterson41.github.io/cinderloft/

Hold Space, W, Up or the mouse to fire the lift jet. Down or S dives. Left and Right steer. P pauses, M mutes.

The whole game is `index.html`, with no build step and no dependencies.
